About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for capturing ideas the moment they come to you. It helps you keep your notes organised and easy to find later.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes in your browser.
  2. Type your note into the input box.
  3. Click the Add Note button.
  4. Read your saved notes in the list below.

Features

  • Add notes in seconds
  • See all your notes in one list
  • Simple, distraction-free design
